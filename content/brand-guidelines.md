# StreamArtisan Brand Voice Guidelines

## Brand Name Standards:
- **Correct:** StreamArtisan (one word, camelCase)
- **Incorrect:** Stream Artisan, stream artisan, Streamartisan

## Tone: Professional yet approachable
- Use "we" instead of "our experts" or "our team"
- Avoid technical jargon in client-facing content
- Focus on business outcomes, not technical features

## Messaging Hierarchy:
1. Problem identification
2. Solution presentation  
3. Proof/credibility
4. Clear call-to-action

## Content Standards:
- Company name: "StreamArtisan" (consistent across all pages)
- Email: services@streamartisan.com
- Website: https://streamartisan.com
- Tagline: "Crafting Digital Excellence"
